How a pickup actually happens

No forms, no accounts. The whole handoff runs inside a WhatsApp thread.

1 edit_note

You text what you have

Quantity and food type, in plain language — however you'd normally describe it.

2 smart_toy

AI reads and verifies it

Amazon Bedrock classifies the message and screens out spam before anything is dispatched.

3 near_me

Nearest driver gets the job

Once you share your location, the closest available volunteer is notified automatically.

4 task_alt

Pickup and drop-off, photographed

The driver photographs the pickup, then the handoff at the community fridge — proof either way.

Join the driver network

Have an hour and a two-wheeler? That's enough.

Pickups are short — one stop for the food, one stop at the fridge. You pick up jobs by replying ACCEPT on WhatsApp whenever you're free; there's no shift to show up for.
